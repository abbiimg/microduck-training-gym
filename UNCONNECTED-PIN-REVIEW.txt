MicroDuck unconnected-pin review — 2026-10-26

Both recovered IMU projects have the same four pins without a recovered net:
U1.3 PC15-OSC32_OUT; U1.20 PB3/PB4/PB5/PB6;
U2.10 OCS_Aux; U2.11 SDO_Aux.
All eight source pinmap entries have not_connected=false, no named nets, and no
wire peers. They are electrically unconnected in recovery but NOT explicit
source no-connect declarations. Pairing does not add no-connect markers.

Datasheet assessment
ST's LSM6DSV16X DS13510 Rev 4 Table 23 (page 50) permits auxiliary pins 10 and 11
to remain open in primary-interface modes 1 and 2. Their internal pull-ups are
controlled by PIN_CTRL.OIS_PU_DIS (page 57). Therefore these four entries across
the two IMUs are conditionally compatible with an unused auxiliary interface;
this is not evidence of the author's intent or of measured pin state.
Verify intended interface mode and register state before release.
https://www.st.com/content/st_com/en/campaigns/resource/en/datasheet/lsm6dsv16x.pdf

ST's STM32G031 DS12992 Rev 4 Figure 5 (page 32) identifies the TSSOP20 package
functions. The reviewed firmware/Core/Src/board.c uses PA5/6/7 for main SPI,
PA2/3 for bus USART, remapped PA9/10 for the log UART, PA1/PA4 and PB7/PB8 for
buffer/CS control. It does not provide a release qualification of the unused
PC15 and combined PB3/4/5/6 package pins. Check oscillator mode, reset/boot state,
GPIO configuration and tied internal pad behavior against the selected firmware
build. Keep these four MCU entries open pending that qualification.
https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32g031j6.pdf

The local generic passive symbols cannot establish electrical ERC correctness.
No shorts, ties or pull resistors were added to make these pins appear resolved.
The firmware and physical-hardware variant still need to be reconciled before
any pin review can be promoted to complete-board acceptance.
