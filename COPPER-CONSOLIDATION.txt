Copper consolidation
editing/kicad-copper-consolidated/ contains five copied paired projects. Saved
copper was unioned only within the same native net and copper layer; disconnected
regions remain separate static zones. Track/via geometry, pad polygons and
drills were compared with the parent and preserved. Native saved-copper XOR
checks are zero for every group. Independent Shapely/GEOS checks confirm that
all differences lie within a 2 nm coordinate band, accounting for KiCad's
integer-grid clipping of intersections. The largest GEOS area difference is
about 2.84e-7 mm2, confined to that band. The union was not refilled or rerouted.
Same-net overlaps were reduced without suppressing DRC checks. Boundary-touch
zone warnings remain and must be assessed, rather than assigned arbitrary
priorities merely to clear the report.

The revised-IMU hypothesis explicitly excludes only the old large F.Cu FILL
84a63625734e769f, identified by its source history, name, layer and area. It
retains the later copper cache. No FILL tombstone has been found. The hypothesis
has not become verified source intent; its geometry-equivalence scope excludes
that one removal. Literal copies continue to preserve it and expose the shorts.

Source manufacturing minima
audit/source-rule-review.txt/.json and source-manufacturing-minima.json record
the installed client decoder/DRC evidence and exact bundle hashes. Static file
inspection requires no activation. The installed decoder divides raw rule
lengths by 10 into 0.01-inch internal units: raw lengths are mil. RADIUS fields
are radii and the installed DRC doubles them for diameter checks. Missing TRACK
layers use reference layer 1. All four boards have no TRACK/RADIUS selector
overrides and no named-via overrides.

The engineering .kicad_pro files project these limits on KiCad's nm grid:
  track width min                 0.127000 mm
  via outer diameter min          0.499999 mm
  through-hole/drill diameter min  0.299999 mm
The source via-drill value is 0.2999994 mm before quantization. Rounding it
upward to 0.300000 mm would create 1 nm false findings against the imported
geometry. KiCad's global through-hole minimum also applies to PTH component
pads; this is a bounded projection of the source via limit, not a reconstruction
of every possible source hole rule. No existing PTH violation is hidden by this
1 nm correction. Board files are BYTE-IDENTICAL to the consolidated parents;
only the project constraints change.

The latest published schema conflicts with this installed version's decoder
unit convention. Preserve that version distinction. SAFE is a pair-class
matrix, not one global clearance. Its indices and evidence are documented, but
full native pair-class/selector translation remains incomplete. Mask/paste,
edge and other clearances, maximum widths and default routing sizes are not
fully translated. Do not weaken them based on plausible-looking numbers.

Battery copper rings
The active source contains two F.Cu POLY NORMAL circles with empty netName;
the earlier converter represented their four halves as PCB_ARC tracks. Source
graphics use a different clearance class from tracks. Their ~0.1 mm strokes
therefore acquired four inappropriate track-width and four dangling-track
findings. They are not isolated: each ring intersects two same-net 1.016 mm
traces, which reach the PTH pads. The small gap from ring directly to PTH copper
does not break that trace connection. audit/pogo-copper-ring-review.txt/.json
records positive overlap areas and source IDs.

restore_pogo_copper_graphics.py creates four net-assigned native gr_arc items,
preserving exact saved start/mid/end, width, layer and parent derived net name.
Pads and five trace segments are unchanged. No bridge or extra copper is added.
The corrected copy passes native DRC/parity with zero findings. Its fabrication
dimensions, solder mask, connector fit and electrical acceptance remain open.

PCB-mapped buffer functional slice
The eight physical SN74LVC2G241DCUR U3 pins and recovered PCB nets were reviewed
against TI's datasheet. Two unchanged vendor active-high stages are placed in
an authored eight-port wrapper. RX maps directly; TX enable uses an explicitly
ideal algebraic inverter to model its active-low logical function.
test_ti_buffer_package.py checks the recovered/native pin maps, R4/R5/R7 10k
pull-ups and placed R6=0R, keeping DXL_DATA/DXL_BUS as separate nodes connected
by a zero-volt link. All 12 cases and 22 voltage assertions pass: TX/RX logic,
disabled-state fixture pulls, reset TX-off/RX-on bias, and TX-to-bus-to-RX loops.
CPU and external bus signals are fixed-voltage mocks.

Scope is fixed 3.3 V and enable voltages within 0..VCC. The ideal inversion
does not qualify physical input capacitance, enable timing or shared package
current. No power-off, overvoltage/back-drive, corner, contention, whole-board
or hardware result is implied. Original TI model hash/notices are preserved.
Read audit/buffer-package-review.txt and the wrapper comments before reuse.
