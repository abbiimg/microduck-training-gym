Source objects and classification
The source contains TWO F.Cu POLY records, each describing a closed circle as
two 180-degree arcs. They are not four separate source tracks:
  019b7888d641f854 (near GND/U2)
  11df8aac0dd30763 (near Battery/U1)
Both have polyType=NORMAL and EMPTY netName. Each stroke is 3.937 mil, or
0.0999998 mm; centerline radius is approximately 0.850000 mm.
The installed source DRC maps a non-outline POLY/Line graphic to SAFE class 9,
whereas an actual source track has class 0 and the TRACK minimum-width rule.

Current converter output
build_routed_kicad.py turns each arc segment into native PCB_ARC track items,
resulting in four track objects. Their current native nets are /GND and /Battery;
those assignments are not explicit source POLY net labels. They reflect the
connected copper context. Both arcs within each circle have exactly matching
start/end coordinates after save, so there is no nanometre endpoint gap between
the two halves. DRC still emits four track_dangling and four track_width checks.

Physical contact assessment
The GND circle center is (76.38299938, -9.78899998) mm. Its copper is separated
from matching U2 F.Cu PTH copper by approximately 0.098788 mm. Battery circle
center is (110.18300163, -9.78899998) mm, with an approximately 0.097681 mm gap
to matching U1 F.Cu PTH copper. Thus neither ring directly touches the matching
small top annular pad.

However, neither ring is an isolated copper island. Each positively intersects
both of the same-net 40-mil (1.016 mm) F.Cu track polygons running into the
corresponding PTH center. Per-track overlap areas are roughly 0.109–0.111 mm².
The broad track enters the center/annular-pad area and gives the ring physical
contact to the same net. The 3.3 mm B.Cu disk at each hole overlaps the ring in
plan view, but layer separation means that planar overlap is not direct F.Cu
contact; the routed PTH connection is what matters. Other-net tracks are tens
of millimetres away from each ring.

Recommendation
Represent the source POLY circles as native copper graphics (PCB_SHAPE gr_arc
or a gr_circle), rather than PCB_ARC tracks. This restores the source graphic
classification; it does not add a bridge across the pad-to-ring gaps. Preserve
the original centerline/stroke geometry. If native graphic net assignment is
used, document it as derived from the already touching /GND or /Battery copper,
not as an explicit source POLY net name.

Changing classification removes inappropriate TRACK-width and track-end tests
from these graphics. It is justified by the source type and verified positive
trace contact, rather than merely hiding a physical discontinuity. Still verify
native copper XOR/area, unchanged physical track-to-pad continuity, zero
unconnected items, and different-net/edge clearances after conversion. Do not
widen the ring or move source geometry merely to make a track-only DRC pass.

Remaining qualification
These measurements do not establish mechanical hole tolerance, annular-ring
manufacturability, full mask apertures, current capacity, original author intent,
or PCB readiness for fabrication. The unplaced CN2 and other package-level
qualification gates remain separate.
