Remaining conversion work
- Copper caches/teardrops are STATIC filled zones, not regenerated KiCad pours.
  Do not refill zones or assume the cached geometry follows subsequent routing.
  Pad connection FULL describes precomputed cache geometry; original thermal
  shapes are already in the cached polygons. KiCad must not infer spokes again.
- Same-net overlapping teardrop zones account for 26/102 zone-intersection
  warnings in older/revised alternate IMUs. Original constraints, net classes,
  solder-mask/paste settings and copper priorities are not faithfully translated.
  Default KiCad clearance/hole limits also differ from the source. No design
  rule was weakened or DRC finding suppressed to obtain a pass.
- Non-pad footprint graphics, text/silkscreen, courtyards, source 3D placement,
  noncopper fills and per-layer specialPad overrides are not fully reconstructed.
  conversion-audit.json inventories omissions. Supplier 3D candidates elsewhere
  in the package are not automatically attached to these source footprints.
- Review schematics and routed boards are not linked by footprint UUID paths.
  Do not run Update PCB from Schematic until a deliberate pairing/migration is
  completed; generic candidate footprints can replace recovered source geometry.
- Qualify dimensions, mask apertures, drills, connector heights, copper current
  capacity and every remaining DRC item against datasheets/source drawings.
  No Gerber release or PCB purchase is justified by these results.
