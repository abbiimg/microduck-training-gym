How to regenerate and verify
Use WSL NVIDIA-Workbench and /home/workbench/robotics/pcb-tools/venv/bin/python:
  tools/pair_kicad_projects.py
  tools/verify_paired_projects.py
  tools/verify_recovered_libraries.py
Run generators only before making user edits: they overwrite generated projects.
Pairing assumes the saved recovered schematics and routed/engineering parents.
tools/run_software_checks.py is the Windows entry point for the combined suite.
Its paired-project step checks geometry and schematic parity; it deliberately
does NOT treat unresolved physical DRC violations as a software-release pass.

Boundaries
Native parity and library reload have been tested. An actual interactive
Update PCB from Schematic operation has not been tested; first perform it on
another copy and review its proposed changes. Do not refill static copper zones.
Recover regenerative pours/net classes and verify mask/paste settings before
using the layouts as production design templates.

The paired revised IMU deliberately retains literal typed source replay, including
the unresolved large copper-fill history ambiguity. It is NOT the alternate
transition-hypothesis board. Its parity pass does not imply that copper is safe.
DRC cleanup, source graphics/courtyards/3D placement, qualified symbol pin types,
source-rule translation and manufacturing checks remain open. Read
ROUTED-PCB-RECOVERY.txt for the literal/alternate distinction and DRC evidence.
