Important BOM correction
The reader's raw BOM/component commands can expose DEVICE-library defaults and
miss placed-component overrides. R6's library says 150 ohm/C22808, but the actual
v2 placed attributes explicitly say Value=0R, Manufacturer Part=0603WAF0000T5E,
Supplier Part=C21189. That agrees with the author README. Do NOT procure from
raw bom.json alone. Each recovered project now has effective-bom.json and
instance-attributes.json, generated by tools/reconcile_instance_bom.py.
KiCad reconstruction now preserves those placed values. No source file was edited.

Remaining release gates, in order
1. Faithfully reconstruct the routed native KiCad PCB layouts, pads, drills,
   zones and outlines and verify them against recovered source geometry/nets.
   The strict geometry reader now gets past EDIT_HEAD metadata but rejects
   this outline dialect: UNSUPPORTED_BOARD_OUTLINE, expecting an explicit
   BOARD_OUTLINE polygon. No geometry was guessed to force a pass.
2. Validate component values, datasheet pin types, electrical models and
   footprint dimensions; resolve eight unnamed/unmarked IMU pins. Simulate
   supply/load/transient/protection/bus behavior with coverage for every part.
3. Resolve the HAT reference's existing DRC/ERC findings; generic-symbol ERC
   on the recovered boards is not equivalent evidence.
4. Freeze one consistent physical variant: official simulation has 14 actions;
   community Feetech firmware differs from the XL330 CAD/servo version, and
   some hardware has a 15th mouth servo. Do not silently combine those variants.
5. Close mechanical fit and complete BOM/harness/part availability: original
   Pollen parametric CAD remains unavailable; community CAD is reconstructed.
   Hub CN1 has documented 8.4mm versus approximately 5mm space interference.
6. Qualify physical supply, temperature, signal timing, sensor axes/fusion,
   connectors and assembly tolerances on a bench fixture. CPU mocks cannot
   prove these. No physical hardware was operated or flashed.
7. Match CAD-derived mass/inertia/actuator properties to the chosen simulation
   variant, then run full training and evaluate a policy against explicit
   locomotion goals. The new short run is only a pipeline check.
