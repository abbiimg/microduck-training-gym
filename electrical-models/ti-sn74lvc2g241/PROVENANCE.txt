IMPORTANT: .SUBCKT has five logical terminals: Y A OEZ VCC AGND.
It is ONE active-high-enable channel, despite the ambiguous OEZ terminal label.
Physical SN74LVC2G241 has EIGHT pins and TWO channels:
1=1OE active low, 2=1A, 3=2Y, 4=GND, 5=2A, 6=1Y, 7=2OE active high, 8=VCC.
The provided model is not an eight-pin drop-in package model. A second-channel
wrapper would need reviewed enable inversion and timing behavior. Neither the
full-package mapping nor the actual PCB mapping has been qualified.
